02 / Two meanings of cycle

A peptide cycle and a menstrual cycle are not the same thing.

A “peptide cycle” may mean a protocol or schedule. A menstrual cycle is the biological menstrual cycle. They describe different things, even when the same word is used.

Revé keeps your routine records alongside menstrual-cycle context without treating them as interchangeable. Seeing both in your history is not a reason to change a medication, compound, dose, or protocol.
