What is a supplement tracker for women?

A supplement tracker for women keeps the supplements you already take next to the check-ins you choose to log. Revé records doses, timing, schedules, and reminders, and tags each logged entry with your cycle phase. It does not tell you which supplement belongs in which phase.

01 / Why the phase stays on the entry

Why tag a supplement entry with your cycle phase?

A date says when you wrote the note. Cycle phase says where that day sat in your cycle. With the phase on the entry, a note from this cycle can sit next to your note from the same phase of a previous cycle. You are reading your own log.

That lineup is for review. It is not a reason to start, stop, or switch a supplement. Revé does not suggest what to take in any phase.

Doses, timing, and schedules

Build a routine with the supplements you already take, including doses, timing, and schedules. Log each dose as you take it, and set a reminder time if you want one.

Cycle phase on the entry

Every dose, check-in, and measurement you log is tagged with your cycle phase. That label is what lets you line up the same phase across cycles.

Check-ins you choose

Record mood, energy, sleep, appetite, or any changes you notice, if you want a check-in. A daily check-in takes 47 seconds. These are observations you choose to log.

Measurements and photos

Track measurements and progress photos over time, then compare your logs phase by phase and cycle to cycle.

The tag is there so you can reread your own notes.

Same phase, another cycle, your own words beside each other. Revé does not explain what you wrote, and it does not tell you what to take next.

04 / A few answers

Questions about a supplement log for women.

What can I log in a supplement routine?

You can log supplements you already take, with doses, timing, and schedules, and set a reminder time. You choose what else to record: mood, energy, sleep, appetite, and any changes you notice. Revé does not tell you what to take.

Why is each supplement entry tagged with cycle phase?

So you can line your own notes up by phase. A note from this cycle can sit next to your note from the same phase of an earlier cycle. The tag is context for that review. It is not a suggestion to take a supplement in that phase.

How is Revé different from a supplement checklist?

A checklist marks a dose as done. Revé keeps the dose, timing, schedule, cycle phase, and any check-in, measurement, or progress photo you choose to log. You can compare that record phase by phase and cycle to cycle. Revé does not recommend supplements.

Can I log peptides and supplements in one routine?

Yes. Peptides and supplements can sit in one routine, and each logged entry is tagged with your cycle phase. Keeping them together does not recommend a combination or tell you what to take.

Does Revé recommend supplements for a cycle phase?

No. Revé recommends nothing. It logs what you already take and tags each entry with your cycle phase. It does not choose a supplement, set a dose, or change your schedule. It sells no compounds.
