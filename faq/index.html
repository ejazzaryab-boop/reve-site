Frequently Asked Questions

Questions about Revé

What is the best way to track peptide doses?

A consistent tracking system can make it easier to keep your routine organized and review your history over time. Revé lets you keep your peptide tracking and related check-ins together in one place.

Is Revé made specifically for women?

Yes. Revé is designed around women's tracking needs and includes menstrual-cycle context alongside peptide, supplement, symptom, and progress tracking.

Can I track peptides and my menstrual cycle together?

Yes. Revé lets you keep your peptide tracking alongside menstrual-cycle information so you can review your own entries with cycle context.

Does Revé track GLP-1 routines too?

Revé can be used to organize supported peptide and GLP-1 routines alongside your symptoms, progress, and other check-ins.

Can I track supplements alongside peptides?

Yes. Revé is designed to keep peptide and supplement tracking together so related routines do not have to be split across separate notes or apps.

Does Revé tell me when or how much peptide to take?

No. Revé is a personal tracking tool. It helps you organize and review the information you log, but it does not prescribe peptides, recommend doses, or tell you to change a protocol based on your menstrual cycle.

For a free paper log, the peptide and cycle log template is a printable sheet with cycle day and cycle phase. Revé is the app for keeping that kind of log on your phone.
