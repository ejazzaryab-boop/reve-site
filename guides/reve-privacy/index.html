What Revé stores, who can see it, and how to delete it

What Revé keeps, who receives it, and how deletion works, from the privacy policy last updated October 20, 2026 and the App Store privacy label.

By the Revé editorial team · Updated Nov 22, 2026

Short answer

Revé stores your email and name, Sign in with Apple or Google without receiving passwords, period start dates and calculated phases, compounds and protocols, dose logs and timing, journal notes, moods, symptoms, measurements, and progress photos private to your account. It does not sell this data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, or use health data for advertising. Delete it in Settings > Account. Deletion is permanent and immediate, so export your data in the app first.

What Revé stores

Revé stores the account details you sign in with, and the logs you keep in the app.

Account details are your email address and your name, from Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. Revé does not receive your Apple or Google password.

The app also stores:

  • Period start dates, and the phases calculated from them
  • Compounds and protocols
  • Dose logs and timing
  • Journal notes, moods, and symptoms
  • Measurements
  • Progress photos, which are private to your account

That list is from the Revé privacy policy, last updated October 20, 2026.

Where your data is hosted

Supabase hosts your Revé data. Servers may be outside your country.

What Revé does not do with your data

Revé does not sell your data. It does not share your data with advertisers or data brokers. It does not use health data for advertising.

Revé does not track you across other apps or websites. On the App Store listing, the App Privacy label does not list Data Used to Track You.

How to delete your account

In the app, open Settings > Account. Deletion is permanent and immediate.

Export your data in the app first. Deletion is permanent and immediate.

After you delete your account, Revé keeps a minimal referral record for subscription accounting. That record has no name, no email, and no health data.

Who can use Revé

Revé is for people 18 and older. The App Store listing shows an age rating of 18+.

Who operates Revé

Revé's legal name is Trendset Enterprises LLC. On the App Store, the developer is listed as Reve Health, and the seller is Trendset Enterprises LLC.

Questions

Questions about Revé and your data

Does Reve sell my data?

No. Revé does not sell your data. It does not share your data with advertisers or data brokers, and it does not use health data for advertising. The privacy policy that says this was last updated October 20, 2026.

Can I delete my Reve account?

Yes. In the app, open Settings > Account. Deletion is permanent and immediate, so export your data in the app first. After that, Revé keeps a minimal referral record for subscription accounting, with no name, no email, and no health data.

Where is my data stored?

Supabase hosts your Revé data. Servers may be outside your country. Progress photos are part of what is stored, and they are private to your account.

Does Reve use my health data for advertising?

No. Revé does not use health data for advertising. It also does not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Does Reve track me across other apps?

No. Revé does not track you across other apps or websites. The App Privacy section of the App Store listing does not list Data Used to Track You.

Does Reve get my Apple or Google password?

No. If you use Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google, Revé stores your email and name and does not receive your password. Apple and Google are used for sign-in only.

Do I have to be 18 to use Reve?

Yes. Revé is for people 18 and older. The App Store listing shows an age rating of 18+.
