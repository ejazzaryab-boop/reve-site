How much does Revé cost? US App Store prices

Revé is free to download on the App Store. A subscription is required to use the app: Monthly $24.99 or 3 Months $69.99 in the US.

By the Revé editorial team · Updated Nov 21, 2026

Short answer

Revé is free to download on the App Store, and a subscription is required to use the app. In the US, Monthly is $24.99, billed every month, and 3 Months is $69.99, billed every 3 months (about $23.33 a month, $4.98 less than three monthly payments). The paywall appears during onboarding. There is no free trial on the standard paywall. Prices in other countries may differ. Revé is iPhone only, on iOS 17 or later, with no Android app.

The US App Store has two plans:

How the subscription renews

Both plans auto-renew until cancelled. Monthly is billed every month. 3 Months is billed every 3 months.

How to cancel

Cancel in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends.
