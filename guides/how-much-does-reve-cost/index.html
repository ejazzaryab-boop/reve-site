How much does Revé cost? US App Store prices

What that US App Store subscription includes, and how to cancel it.

By the Revé editorial team · Updated Nov 23, 2026

Short answer

Revé is free to download on the App Store, and a subscription is required to use the app. In the US, Monthly is $24.99, billed every month, and 3 Months is $69.99, billed every 3 months (about $23.33 a month, $4.98 less than three monthly payments).

Onboarding

The paywall appears during onboarding.

How to cancel

The subscription auto-renews until cancelled. Cancel in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends.

What the subscription includes

The US App Store listing, checked Nov 23, 2026, describes these features:

  • Peptides and supplements, doses, timing, and schedules in one place
  • Dose logs as you take them, with reminders
  • Each logged dose tagged to your cycle phase
  • Records of mood, energy, sleep, appetite, and other changes you notice
  • Measurements and progress photos over time
  • Comparisons of your logs phase by phase and cycle to cycle
  • Plain language reference material on common compounds

iPhone only

Revé is iPhone only and requires iOS 17 or later. There is no Android app.

The FAQ and the homepage are on this site.

Questions

Questions about what Revé costs

Who do I pay for Revé?

You pay Apple, and it is charged to your Apple ID. The seller on the App Store is Trendset Enterprises LLC.

What is the developer name on the App Store?

The developer is listed as Reve Health. More on the company is in Is Revé legit?.

Does Revé have a free trial?

No. There is no free trial on the standard paywall.

Is the Revé price the same in every country?

Prices in other countries may differ. The figures on this page are the US App Store prices.
