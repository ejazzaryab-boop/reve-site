Can Clue track peptides? Not built in, but you can log by hand

What Clue's help center says about custom tags, daily notes, and reminders, checked Nov 25, 2026, and when an iPhone log built for peptides is a closer fit.

By the Revé editorial team · Updated Nov 25, 2026

Short answer

Clue has no built-in peptide log. Checked Nov 25, 2026, Clue's help pages say you can record an entry that is not already a tracking option by writing a custom tag, or a daily note when Clue Plus is enabled. A what's-new note on the same help center adds one named option under Medications, and that note does not describe a dose, a time, a schedule, or a cycle phase on the entry.

What Clue's help pages say

Clue's help center, checked Nov 25, 2026, does not describe a built-in peptide log. How to personalize tracking with custom tags and notes says that if you want a tracking option that is not already in the app, you use Daily Note and Tags. It calls tags custom tracking options for anything you would like to track that is not included in the available options. It calls a daily note a personalized note on your daily tracking.

How can I customize Clue to fit my unique needs? says the same thing in short form. If the default options do not cover what you want to track, you create a custom tag. Daily Notes, which that article says are included with Clue Plus, are for any extra detail.

What's new in the Clue app? adds one named option in the tracking category Medications. That line does not describe a peptide log. It does not describe a dose amount, a clock time, a schedule, or a cycle phase stored on the entry. When the thing you want to track is still not an option, the custom tag article is the path Clue documents.

How to log with custom tags

These steps are only the ones in Clue's custom tag article, checked Nov 25, 2026.

Turn Tags on:

  1. Tap the Track button at the bottom of the screen.
  2. Tap Customize at the top-right.
  3. Scroll to the Tags option. The article says it sits in the middle of the list by default, and that you can move it.
  4. Toggle Tags on.
  5. Go back to the tracking screen. The article says you then see My tags.

Create a tag:

  1. Tap Track and scroll to My tags.
  2. Tap Create new tag.
  3. Write the tag.
  4. Confirm with Done at the bottom right of the keyboard on iOS, or with the check mark in that spot on Android.
  5. The article says the tag then appears in the list and that you can track it whenever you need.

The customize article's short version is the same idea: go to Track, scroll to My Tags, and tap Create New Tag.

How to log with a daily note

Clue's tag article says Daily Notes have to be turned on, and the first step is to enable Clue Plus on the device. The customize article also says Daily Notes are included with Clue Plus.

Turn Daily Note on:

  1. Enable Clue Plus on the device.
  2. Tap Track at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Tap Customize at the top-right.
  4. Scroll to Daily Note.
  5. Toggle it on.

Write the note:

  1. Tap Track and scroll to Daily Note.
  2. Write the note for the day.
  3. Confirm with Done on iOS, or with the check mark on Android.
  4. The article says you then see the note on the day you are currently on in the Clue Calendar.

What tags and notes do not do

The tag and note articles describe a label you can track and a note for that calendar day. Those articles do not describe an amount, a clock time, a schedule, or a cycle phase saved on the entry.

On reminders, Can I create my own reminder? says it is not possible to create your own unique reminder in the Clue app. What reminders are available in the Clue app? lists Period starting soon, Period due, Period late, Fertile window soon (that article says this one is part of Clue Plus), a minipill reminder for when it is time to take your birth control pill, Cycle statistic changes, and Hi from Clue, which it calls a daily check-in. It says you can set the schedule, the sending time, and your own message for those reminders. The US App Store listing, in the section about getting ahead of symptoms, uses the words "Set custom reminders." The help center is the page that names the reminders and says a unique reminder of your own is not available.

How can I learn about my different cycle phases? describes Cycle Phase Insights as an overview of the phase you are in now. It names six hormonal subphases: early follicular, late follicular, ovulation, early luteal, mid luteal, and late luteal. It says you only see the current phase, not past or future phases. It also describes a reminder for when you have entered a new cycle phase. It says the feature is only in Clue Period Tracking mode for now, and that it stays hidden if a hormonal birth control method is set in your health profile. The article does not say a tag or a note is stored with a phase name.

What kind of data is shown in the report? describes the Doctor's Report. It says the report can include optional fields you fill in for a visit, including medications, and that a heatmap shows tracking from the last 6 cycles for experiences you tracked 3 or more times. It says the heatmap leaves out single-event categories. That list includes Medications and Daily notes. The list does not name custom tags, so this page does not claim a tag is on that graph or off it. The heatmap is not described as a phase label on each dose.

How can I filter my tracking data in the Calendar View? says you can select up to 3 experiences you have tracked and see an icon on each date you tracked them. What's included in Clue Plus? lists calendar filters as a Clue Plus feature. The filter article describes dates on a calendar. It does not describe a dose schedule compared phase by phase.

Clue is the closer fit when you want that period and cycle tracker, the Android app its help center documents, birth control logging, and the birth control pill reminder, and a hand-written tag or note is enough for something Clue does not already offer as a tracking option.

Questions

Questions about logging this in Clue

Is there a limit on custom tags in Clue?

Clue's what's-new article says iOS release 303.0 and Android release 279.0 include unlimited custom tags for everyone. The same article says you can search tracking options instead of scrolling through all 250 of them, and that the search example is to type an option such as cramps or to create a tag.

If I delete a custom tag, do I keep the old entries?

No. Clue's custom tag article says you delete a tag by tapping the X next to it. If you have tracked that tag before, deleting it also removes every past use of the tag, and Clue says that tracked data cannot be recovered.

Does Clue Plus add a peptide log?

Clue's Clue Plus article does not describe a peptide log, a dose, or a dose schedule. It lists premium features such as longer cycle predictions, extra analysis, and 10 premium tracking categories, and it names Supplements as one of those categories. It says you pick a monthly or yearly plan and confirm it with the Apple App Store or Google Play.

Where do I see what Clue or Revé costs?

This page does not list prices. Revé's US subscription prices are on How much does Revé cost?. Clue's current in-app purchase prices are on its US App Store listing.

Will Clue or Revé tell me what to take?

No. Clue's US listing says Clue should not be used as a contraceptive and does not substitute professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Revé's US listing says Revé is a personal logging tool that does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and that it does not sell or supply any compound.

Is Clue listed as a medical device?

Clue's US listing says BioWink GmbH indicated the app is a regulated medical device, and the listing says Apple has not verified that indication. The use statement on that listing says Clue is for understanding menstrual and reproductive health by tracking data, and that Clue Conceive mode predicts and displays fertile days as an aid for conception.
