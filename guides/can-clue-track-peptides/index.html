Can Clue track peptides? Not built in, but you can log by hand

Checked Nov 25, 2026. What you can log in Clue by hand, and what a peptide tracker logs instead.

By the Revé editorial team · Updated Nov 25, 2026

Short answer

No. Clue has no peptide log built in. You can write a custom tag, or a daily note when Clue Plus is on. A tag is a label you track on a day. A daily note is text for that day. Revé logs the dose, the time, the schedule, a reminder, and the cycle phase on each entry.

How to log it in Clue

From Clue's custom tag article. The customize article gives the short path: Track, My Tags, Create New Tag.

Add a tag:

  1. Tap Track at the bottom of the screen, then Customize at the top right.
  2. Turn Tags on. It sits in the middle of the list by default, and you can move it.
  3. Go back to Track, open My tags, and tap Create new tag.
  4. Write the tag. Confirm with Done on iOS, or with the check mark on Android.

The tag then appears in the list, and you can track it when you need it. To remove it, tap the X next to the tag. If you have used that tag before, Clue deletes every past use, and that data cannot be recovered.

What's new says iOS 303.0 and Android 279.0 include unlimited custom tags for everyone. You can search instead of scrolling through 250 tracking options. Update Android through Google Play. iOS 303.0 needs iOS 18 or later.

Add a daily note:

  1. Turn on Clue Plus.
  2. Open Track, then Customize, and turn Daily Note on.
  3. On Track, open Daily Note, write the note for that day, and confirm it the same way as a tag.

You then see the note on the day you have open in the calendar.

Reminders and cycle phase

You cannot create your own reminder in Clue. The reminders you can use are Period starting soon, Period due, Period late, Fertile window soon (Clue Plus), a reminder for your birth control pill, Cycle statistic changes, and Hi from Clue, a daily check-in. You can set the schedule, the time, and the message.

Cycle Phase Insights shows the phase you are in now, and Clue can remind you when a new phase starts. It is in Period Tracking mode, and it stays hidden if a hormonal birth control method is set in your health profile.

Questions

Questions about logging this in Clue

Is there a limit on custom tags in Clue?

No. Clue's what's new article says iOS 303.0 and Android 279.0 include unlimited custom tags for everyone. You can search the tracking options instead of scrolling through all 250. The example is to type something like cramps, or to create a tag.

If I delete a custom tag, do I keep the old entries?

No. Tap the X next to the tag to delete it. If you have tracked that tag before, Clue also removes every past use, and it says that tracked data cannot be recovered.

Does Clue Plus add a peptide log?

No. Clue Plus adds Daily Notes, longer cycle predictions, extra analysis, and 10 premium tracking categories, including Supplements. If what you want is still not a tracking option, you use a custom tag or a daily note. You pick a monthly or yearly plan and confirm it in the App Store or on Google Play.
