Is Revé legit? Who makes it, and which Reve it isn't

The facts about Revé in one place: the company behind it, what the app does and doesn't do, what it costs, and how to tell it apart from the other products called Reve.

By the Revé editorial team · Updated Nov 18, 2026

Short answer

Yes, Revé is a real app from a registered company. It's an iPhone app listed on the App Store as "Reve: Peptide & Cycle Tracker", designed and operated by Trendset Enterprises LLC, a limited liability company in New York. It logs peptides, supplements, GLP-1 routines, check-ins and measurements, and tags each entry with your cycle day and phase. It's a free download with a subscription for full access.

Revé does not sell peptides, does not recommend doses, and is not medical advice. It is not related to Reve AI, the image generator at reve.com, or to REVE Chat.

What Revé is

Revé is an iPhone app for women who take peptides, supplements or GLP-1 medications and want to read their logs against their menstrual cycle. You log a dose or a check-in, and Revé stamps it with your cycle day and phase: Menstrual, Follicular, Ovulatory or Luteal. Over a few cycles you can compare the same routine phase by phase and cycle to cycle.

It logs:

  • Peptides and supplements, with doses, timing, schedules and reminders
  • GLP-1 routines
  • Check-ins such as mood, energy, sleep and appetite
  • Measurements and progress photos

Who makes it

Revé is designed and operated by Trendset Enterprises LLC, a limited liability company based in New York, USA. On the App Store, the developer is listed as Reve Health and the seller as Trendset Enterprises LLC. Support and privacy questions go to zareef@usereve.com. The privacy policy and terms live at legal.usereve.com.

What it costs

Revé is a free download. Full access requires a subscription, which is billed and cancelled through your Apple account like any other App Store subscription.

What Revé does not do

  • It doesn't sell anything you put in your body. No peptides, no compounds, no supplements.
  • It doesn't tell you what to take. No dose recommendations, no protocols, no "take more in your luteal phase".
  • It doesn't predict fertility. Cycle phases in Revé are for organizing your notes. They are not for contraception or for planning a pregnancy.
  • It isn't medical advice. It shows you your own logged patterns. Decisions belong with you and your clinician.

How to check for yourself

  1. Open the App Store listing for "Reve: Peptide & Cycle Tracker". The developer should read Reve Health and the seller Trendset Enterprises LLC, and the listing should link to usereve.com.
  2. Read the App Privacy section on the listing and the privacy policy at legal.usereve.com.
  3. Check that any email claiming to be from Revé comes from an @usereve.com address.
  4. If someone sells peptides, "protocols" or coaching under the Revé name, it isn't us. Tell us at zareef@usereve.com.
Questions

Questions about Revé

Is Revé a scam?

No. Revé is an iPhone app published on the App Store and operated by Trendset Enterprises LLC in New York. It is a logging tool: it does not sell peptides or any other compound, and the only thing it sells is its own in-app subscription, billed through Apple.

Who owns Revé?

Revé is designed and operated by Trendset Enterprises LLC, a limited liability company based in New York, USA. On the App Store the developer name is Reve Health. You can reach the team at zareef@usereve.com.

Is Revé the same as Reve AI?

No. Reve AI is a Palo Alto company that makes an AI image generation and editing product at reve.com. Revé (usereve.com) is a women's peptide and cycle tracking app made by Trendset Enterprises LLC. They are unrelated.

Does Revé sell peptides?

No. Revé sells no compounds of any kind. If someone offers to sell you peptides under the Revé name, it is not Revé.

Is Revé free?

Revé is a free download on the App Store. A subscription is required for full access, billed through Apple.

Is Revé available on Android?

Not at the moment. Revé is an iPhone app and requires iOS 17 or later.

Does Revé give medical or dosing advice?

No. Revé logs what you and your clinician decide. It does not prescribe, recommend doses, or tell you to change a routine based on your cycle phase.
