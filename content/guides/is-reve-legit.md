## What Revé is

Revé is an iPhone app for women who take peptides, supplements or GLP-1 medications and want to read their logs against their menstrual cycle. You log a dose or a check-in, and Revé stamps it with your cycle day and phase: Menstrual, Follicular, Ovulatory or Luteal. Over a few cycles you can compare the same routine phase by phase and cycle to cycle.

It logs:

- Peptides and supplements, with doses, timing, schedules and reminders
- GLP-1 routines
- Check-ins such as mood, energy, sleep and appetite
- Measurements and progress photos

## Who makes it

Revé is designed and operated by **Trendset Enterprises LLC**, a limited liability company based in New York, USA. On the App Store, the developer is listed as **Reve Health** and the seller as **Trendset Enterprises LLC**. Support and privacy questions go to [zareef@usereve.com](mailto:zareef@usereve.com). The privacy policy and terms live at legal.usereve.com.

## What it costs

Revé is a free download. Full access requires a subscription, which is billed and cancelled through your Apple account like any other App Store subscription.

## What Revé does not do

- **It doesn't sell anything you put in your body.** No peptides, no compounds, no supplements.
- **It doesn't tell you what to take.** No dose recommendations, no protocols, no "take more in your luteal phase".
- **It doesn't predict fertility.** Cycle phases in Revé are for organizing your notes. They are not for contraception or for planning a pregnancy.
- **It isn't medical advice.** It shows you your own logged patterns. Decisions belong with you and your clinician.
