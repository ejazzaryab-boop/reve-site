## What you can log

|  | Flo | Peptide tracker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What you log | A birth control method, or a symptom Flo already includes | Dose, time, and schedule |
| Reminders | Period, ovulation, birth control or medication, and habits | Dose reminders |
| Cycle | Period, ovulation, and fertile day predictions | The cycle phase on each entry |

Flo's column is from its help center. The medication log it describes is a birth control method. The [free version](https://help.flo.health/hc/en-us/articles/4411293934740-What-s-included-in-the-free-version) also reminds you about your period, ovulation, birth control or medication, and a habit such as drinking water.

The peptide tracker column is [Revé's US listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/reve-peptide-cycle-tracker/id6810085933). You log peptides and supplements with a dose, a time, and a schedule, plus dose reminders. Every dose, every check-in, and every measurement is tagged with your cycle phase. A supplement dose is tagged like any other dose. You can also record mood, energy, sleep, appetite, other changes you notice, and progress photos, and compare logs phase by phase and cycle to cycle.

## Birth control reminders

From Flo's [birth control article](https://help.flo.health/hc/en-us/articles/360015106292-How-do-I-log-my-birth-control-method).

1. Open Menu, then Reminders, then Medication and contraception.
2. Choose the type you use, switch it on, make the edits, and save.
3. To mark a pill, tap + on the main screen, then Oral contraceptives, then Taken on time, then Apply. You can log yesterday's pill the same way.

The types in that article are oral contraceptives, a vaginal ring, a contraceptive patch, an injection, an IUD, and an implant. [Flo's glossary](https://help.flo.health/hc/en-us/articles/4406826083860-Flo-glossary) describes these reminders as taking a pill, changing a patch, ring, or implant, getting an injection, or checking the strings of an IUD. The injection in that list is a contraceptive injection. The article does not ask you to enter a dose.

You can turn medication, contraception, and lifestyle notifications on or off under Reminders.

## Symptoms and categories

Flo's [how to use the app](https://help.flo.health/hc/en-us/articles/360014347632-How-do-I-use-the-app) article says there are more than 80 symptoms and events to log. The [US listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flo-cycle-period-tracker/id1038369065) says over 70 trackable symptoms and moods. You tap + on the main screen, select the symptoms or events, and apply them. To change the list, tap Edit next to Categories and switch a category off. The same edit is in Flo's [article on predictions changing](https://help.flo.health/hc/en-us/articles/360015106212-I-logged-a-symptom-and-my-predictions-changed-Why).

That edit switches Flo's own categories on or off. Logged items show on that date, and you can open the calendar to review a past day. Menu, then Graphs and reports, shows an overview of the current and past cycles. Symptom articles Flo publishes include [menstrual flow](https://help.flo.health/hc/en-us/articles/360054540452-How-do-I-log-my-menstrual-flow), [basal body temperature, and ovulation tests](https://help.flo.health/hc/en-us/articles/360015318231-How-do-I-log-my-basal-temperature-or-ovulation-test-results).

Some symptoms you enter can move a prediction. Flo says the more periods and symptoms you log, the more precise those predictions become. On the calendar, predicted period days, fertile days, and the ovulation day are marked in different colors. Stories in the app follow your cycle phase.

## Which app fits

[Flo's US listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/flo-cycle-period-tracker/id1038369065) shows 4.7 stars from 2,032,756 ratings. FLO HEALTH UK LIMITED is the developer and the seller. Flo needs iOS 17.0 or later. It runs on Apple Watch (watchOS 10.0 or later). The age rating on the listing is 13+. The help center also documents an Android app.

The listing describes a period, ovulation, pregnancy, and perimenopause tracker. [Pregnancy mode](https://help.flo.health/hc/en-us/articles/360054523711-What-is-Pregnancy-mode-and-how-do-I-activate-it) shows your gestational age and each pregnancy week. Period and ovulation predictions are in the free version. Flo Premium is an optional subscription, billed by Apple or Google.

Flo is the closer fit for a period, fertility, or pregnancy app, including on Android and Apple Watch.
