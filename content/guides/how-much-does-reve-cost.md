## Free to download, subscription required

Revé is free to download on the App Store. A subscription is required to use the app. The paywall appears during onboarding.

The legal name is Trendset Enterprises LLC. On the App Store the developer is listed as Reve Health. More on who operates Revé is in [Is Revé legit?](/guides/is-reve-legit/).

## The two US plans

The US App Store has two plans:

| Plan | Price | How it bills |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | $24.99 | Billed every month |
| 3 Months | $69.99 | Billed every 3 months |

The 3 Months plan is about $23.33 a month, and $4.98 less than three monthly payments.

## How the subscription renews

Both plans auto-renew until cancelled. Monthly is billed every month. 3 Months is billed every 3 months.

## How to cancel

Cancel in iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before the period ends.

## There is no free trial on the standard paywall

There is no free trial on the standard paywall. The paywall appears during onboarding, and a subscription is required to use the app.

## iPhone only

Revé is iPhone only and requires iOS 17 or later. There is no Android app.

## Prices in other countries

Monthly $24.99 and 3 Months $69.99 are US App Store prices. Prices in other countries may differ.
