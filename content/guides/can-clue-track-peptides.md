## What Clue's help pages say

Clue's help center, checked Nov 25, 2026, does not describe a built-in peptide log. [How to personalize tracking with custom tags and notes](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/28891932652573-How-to-personalize-tracking-with-custom-tags-and-notes) says that if you want a tracking option that is not already in the app, you use Daily Note and Tags. It calls tags custom tracking options for anything you would like to track that is not included in the available options. It calls a daily note a personalized note on your daily tracking.

[How can I customize Clue to fit my unique needs?](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/36736932239901-How-can-I-customize-Clue-to-fit-my-unique-needs) says the same thing in short form. If the default options do not cover what you want to track, you create a custom tag. Daily Notes, which that article says are included with Clue Plus, are for any extra detail.

[What's new in the Clue app?](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/18141097264413-What-s-new-in-the-Clue-app) adds one named option in the tracking category Medications. That line does not describe a peptide log. It does not describe a dose amount, a clock time, a schedule, or a cycle phase stored on the entry. When the thing you want to track is still not an option, the custom tag article is the path Clue documents.

## How to log with custom tags

These steps are only the ones in Clue's custom tag article, checked Nov 25, 2026.

Turn Tags on:

1. Tap the Track button at the bottom of the screen.
2. Tap Customize at the top-right.
3. Scroll to the Tags option. The article says it sits in the middle of the list by default, and that you can move it.
4. Toggle Tags on.
5. Go back to the tracking screen. The article says you then see My tags.

Create a tag:

1. Tap Track and scroll to My tags.
2. Tap Create new tag.
3. Write the tag.
4. Confirm with Done at the bottom right of the keyboard on iOS, or with the check mark in that spot on Android.
5. The article says the tag then appears in the list and that you can track it whenever you need.

The customize article's short version is the same idea: go to Track, scroll to My Tags, and tap Create New Tag.

## How to log with a daily note

Clue's tag article says Daily Notes have to be turned on, and the first step is to enable Clue Plus on the device. The customize article also says Daily Notes are included with Clue Plus.

Turn Daily Note on:

1. Enable Clue Plus on the device.
2. Tap Track at the bottom of the screen.
3. Tap Customize at the top-right.
4. Scroll to Daily Note.
5. Toggle it on.

Write the note:

1. Tap Track and scroll to Daily Note.
2. Write the note for the day.
3. Confirm with Done on iOS, or with the check mark on Android.
4. The article says you then see the note on the day you are currently on in the Clue Calendar.

## What tags and notes do not do

The tag and note articles describe a label you can track and a note for that calendar day. Those articles do not describe an amount, a clock time, a schedule, or a cycle phase saved on the entry.

On reminders, [Can I create my own reminder?](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/14561758756765-Can-I-create-my-own-reminder) says it is not possible to create your own unique reminder in the Clue app. [What reminders are available in the Clue app?](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/14561714219677-What-reminders-are-available-in-the-Clue-app) lists Period starting soon, Period due, Period late, Fertile window soon (that article says this one is part of Clue Plus), a minipill reminder for when it is time to take your birth control pill, Cycle statistic changes, and Hi from Clue, which it calls a daily check-in. It says you can set the schedule, the sending time, and your own message for those reminders. The US App Store listing, in the section about getting ahead of symptoms, uses the words "Set custom reminders." The help center is the page that names the reminders and says a unique reminder of your own is not available.

[How can I learn about my different cycle phases?](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/30091944010141-How-can-I-learn-about-my-different-cycle-phases) describes Cycle Phase Insights as an overview of the phase you are in now. It names six hormonal subphases: early follicular, late follicular, ovulation, early luteal, mid luteal, and late luteal. It says you only see the current phase, not past or future phases. It also describes a reminder for when you have entered a new cycle phase. It says the feature is only in Clue Period Tracking mode for now, and that it stays hidden if a hormonal birth control method is set in your health profile. The article does not say a tag or a note is stored with a phase name.

[What kind of data is shown in the report?](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/37230262486685-What-kind-of-data-is-shown-in-the-report) describes the Doctor's Report. It says the report can include optional fields you fill in for a visit, including medications, and that a heatmap shows tracking from the last 6 cycles for experiences you tracked 3 or more times. It says the heatmap leaves out single-event categories. That list includes Medications and Daily notes. The list does not name custom tags, so this page does not claim a tag is on that graph or off it. The heatmap is not described as a phase label on each dose.

[How can I filter my tracking data in the Calendar View?](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/30196149381789-How-can-I-filter-my-tracking-data-in-the-Calendar-View) says you can select up to 3 experiences you have tracked and see an icon on each date you tracked them. [What's included in Clue Plus?](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/15007279508637-What-s-included-in-Clue-Plus) lists calendar filters as a Clue Plus feature. The filter article describes dates on a calendar. It does not describe a dose schedule compared phase by phase.

## Where Clue fits better than Revé

The [Clue Period & Cycle Tracker listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/clue-period-cycle-tracker/id657189652) on the US App Store, checked Nov 25, 2026, names the developer and the seller as BioWink GmbH. It shows 4.8 stars from 409K ratings. The price model on that page is a free download with in-app purchases. The listing is headed Only for iPhone, it says the app requires iOS 18.0 or later, and the age rating is 13+.

The same listing describes period predictions from your cycle length, flow, and patterns, and logging a birth control method, including the pill, an IUD, a ring, and a patch. Clue's own help pages also document an Android app. The tag article includes an Android confirm button, and the what's-new article tells Android users to update through the Google Play Store. [Revé's US listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/reve-peptide-cycle-tracker/id6810085933), checked the same day, is headed Only for iPhone and says it requires iOS 17.0 or later.

Clue is the closer fit when you want that period and cycle tracker, the Android app its help center documents, birth control logging, and the birth control pill reminder, and a hand-written tag or note is enough for something Clue does not already offer as a tracking option.
