## How to log it in Clue

From Clue's [custom tag article](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/28891932652573-How-to-personalize-tracking-with-custom-tags-and-notes). The [customize article](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/36736932239901-How-can-I-customize-Clue-to-fit-my-unique-needs) gives the short path: Track, My Tags, Create New Tag.

Add a tag:

1. Tap Track at the bottom of the screen, then Customize at the top right.
2. Turn Tags on. It sits in the middle of the list by default, and you can move it.
3. Go back to Track, open My tags, and tap Create new tag.
4. Write the tag. Confirm with Done on iOS, or with the check mark on Android.

The tag then appears in the list, and you can track it when you need it. To remove it, tap the X next to the tag. If you have used that tag before, Clue deletes every past use, and that data cannot be recovered.

[What's new](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/18141097264413-What-s-new-in-the-Clue-app) says iOS 303.0 and Android 279.0 include unlimited custom tags for everyone. You can search instead of scrolling through 250 tracking options. Update Android through Google Play. iOS 303.0 needs iOS 18 or later.

Add a daily note:

1. Turn on Clue Plus.
2. Open Track, then Customize, and turn Daily Note on.
3. On Track, open Daily Note, write the note for that day, and confirm it the same way as a tag.

You then see the note on the day you have open in the calendar.

## Reminders and cycle phase

You [cannot create your own reminder](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/14561758756765-Can-I-create-my-own-reminder) in Clue. The [reminders you can use](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/14561714219677-What-reminders-are-available-in-the-Clue-app) are Period starting soon, Period due, Period late, Fertile window soon (Clue Plus), a reminder for your birth control pill, Cycle statistic changes, and Hi from Clue, a daily check-in. You can set the schedule, the time, and the message.

[Cycle Phase Insights](https://support.helloclue.com/hc/en-us/articles/30091944010141-How-can-I-learn-about-my-different-cycle-phases) shows the phase you are in now, and Clue can remind you when a new phase starts. It is in Period Tracking mode, and it stays hidden if a hormonal birth control method is set in your health profile.
