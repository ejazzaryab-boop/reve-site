Why the phase column is there

Line the same point in each cycle up.

A date tells you when you wrote something down. Cycle day and cycle phase give that row a second label, so a note from one cycle can sit next to the same point in a later cycle. One sheet per cycle, with the same day numbers, is an easy way to read them side by side.

That lineup is for your own reading. Day numbers and phase labels on this sheet are a journaling layout. They are for organizing notes, and they are not for contraception, fertility, or medical use.

On iPhone, Revé keeps a routine next to menstrual-cycle context and tags each logged dose, check-in, and measurement with your cycle phase, so you can compare your logs phase by phase and cycle to cycle. Questions about the app are in the FAQ. The short introduction is on the home page.

How to use the sheet

Fill what you want. Leave the rest blank.

01

Set the rows

Choose how many days to show. Enter a start date if you want the date column filled in. Enter a cycle length if you want cycle day to count up and start again at 1. Leave either blank if you would rather write those by hand.

02

Write only what you choose

Use the routine column for a supplement or routine entry in your own words. Mood, energy, sleep, appetite, and notes are check-ins you may leave blank. The sheet does not tell you what to write or what to take.

03

Print or download

Print the page, or download a CSV to open in a spreadsheet. Both stay on your device. What you type is not uploaded.

The sheet

Your peptide and cycle log.

Fourteen blank rows are ready to print. Add a start date when you want the dates filled in. Cycle length only numbers the cycle-day column.

Optional phase labels

Leave a box blank to skip that label. Days after your last number are labeled Luteal. These labels only organize the grid. They are not for contraception, fertility, or medical use.
