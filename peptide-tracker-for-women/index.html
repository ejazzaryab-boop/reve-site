05 / A few answers

Peptide tracking for women: your questions.

What information can I track with Revé?

You can track peptide doses and routines, supplements, symptoms, and progress in Revé. Check-ins include mood, energy, sleep, and appetite, with menstrual-cycle context alongside your entries.

Can I track peptides and supplements together in Revé?

Yes. Revé keeps peptide and supplement routines together with your symptoms, check-ins, and progress, so you can review your own tracking history in one place.

Can I track symptoms alongside my peptide routine?

Yes. You can log symptoms and check-ins such as mood, energy, sleep, and appetite alongside your routine, then look back at your entries over time.

Does Revé track menstrual-cycle context with peptides?

Yes. Revé lets you view your logged entries alongside your menstrual-cycle phase. This adds context to your records; it does not predict your response to a peptide.

Does Revé recommend peptides or adjust my doses?

No. Revé is a tracking tool, not medical advice. It does not tell you what to take, make automatic dosing decisions, or build or modify your peptide stack. Discuss treatment and dosing with a qualified healthcare professional.
