What is a peptide tracker for women?

A peptide tracker for women keeps the routine you follow next to the check-ins and progress you choose to log. Revé records doses, timing, and schedules, and tags each dose, check-in, and measurement with your menstrual-cycle phase.

01 / Peptide tracking built for women

What goes into the peptide tracking workflow?

The workflow is the routine, the daily check-in, and the progress you decide to keep. Cycle phase stays on the entry.

Daily check-ins

Record how you feel, if you want to: mood, energy, sleep, appetite, and any changes you notice. A daily check-in takes 47 seconds.

Measurements & photos

Cycle-phase context

Reminders, daily check-ins, measurements, and cycle phase stay with the routine, so you can look back at what you actually logged.

03 / Menstrual-cycle context

How does cycle phase stay with each peptide log?

Each dose, check-in, and measurement is tagged with your cycle phase. That tag is what lets you compare logs phase by phase and cycle to cycle.

A peptide schedule is different from a menstrual cycle. Tracking them together across a 90-day, three-cycle window adds context to your records; it is not a recommendation to change your doses.

05 / A few answers

Questions about the peptide tracking workflow.

What can I log in a peptide routine?

You can log peptide and supplement doses, timing, and schedules, and set a reminder time. A daily check-in takes 47 seconds. You choose what to record: mood, energy, sleep, appetite, and any changes you notice.

Can I track peptides and supplements together in Revé?

Yes. Peptide and supplement logs stay in one routine record. Keeping them together does not recommend a combination or tell you what to take.

Can I compare progress across cycles?

Yes. You can track measurements and progress photos over time, and compare your logs phase by phase and cycle to cycle. Your 90-day goal comes back at day 30, 60, and 90.

Does Revé track menstrual-cycle context with peptides?

Yes. Every dose, check-in, and measurement you log is tagged with your cycle phase. You can compare those logs phase by phase and cycle to cycle. The tag adds context to the record. It is not a recommendation to change your doses.

Does Revé change my doses or schedule?

No. Revé keeps the routine, reminders, and check-ins you log. It does not tell you what to take, set a dose for you, or change your schedule. Discuss treatment and dosing with a qualified healthcare professional.
